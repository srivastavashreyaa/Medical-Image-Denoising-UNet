# Sample image attribution

These chest X-rays come from the public COVID-19 image data collection (J. P. Cohen et al., https://github.com/ieee8023/covid-chestxray-dataset, arXiv:2003.11597), which aggregates images from several sources. Each image below is licensed **CC BY 3.0** by its original source and was resized to 256x256 grayscale. They were held out from training (validation split).
